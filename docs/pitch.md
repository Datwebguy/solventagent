# Solvent — stream pitch and demo script (15 minutes)

Live site: https://solvent-delta.vercel.app · Code: https://github.com/Datwebguy/solventagent

The judges ask four things. Each section below answers one. Times are targets.

---

## 1. Founder and team (2 min)

- Who you are. One line on what you've built before. Mention the ANSEM game you shipped in July (The Black Bull Charge), which had a Jupiter swap built in.
- Why this problem: "I kept seeing agents on ClawPump earn real money and still depend on their creator to pay their AI bills. Nobody could tell which agents were actually businesses."

## 2. Product and live demo (6 min)

**The one-liner:** "Solvent lets an AI agent pay for its own thinking out of the money it earns, with rules it can't break and records anyone can check."

Demo, in this order, all on the live site:

1. **The top of the page.** "This is Solvent's own money, live. It says SOLVENT, with days of thinking left. Every number has proof behind it."
2. **Budget rules.** "Every dollar it earns is split like this, and these rules are published on Solana. Click 'view proof'. It can never spend more than $5 at once or $10 a day. When money runs low it switches to cheaper AI, and when it runs out it pauses instead of overspending."
3. **Every payment.** "Here is every payment it has made. The green check means your own browser just confirmed none of these records were changed." Point at:
   - "sold a written report": its first real income.
   - "Added to budget, paid in ANSEM": it paid for its own thinking with $ANSEM.
4. **The $ANSEM moment (live).** Send a small amount of $ANSEM with Phantom in the "Buy Solvent some thinking time" box. "That's $ANSEM turning directly into AI thinking. It's a new use for $ANSEM, happening right now."
5. **Clawrena earnings ranking.** "We read what every AnsemHack agent really earns from the public blockchain. Here's who actually makes money." Click **Check** on one.
6. **The paid report.** Click "Get a written report · $0.05", pay with Phantom, and show the report. "Solvent just earned money, and it paid an AI to write that report out of its own budget. Other AI agents can buy these on their own, with no human involved."

## 3. Market, go-to-market, traction (4 min)

- **The market:** ClawPump alone has about 20,000 agents earning token fees, and 183 teams tokenized in this hackathon. Off-chain, Polsia shows that running companies with AI agents is a real business: about $10M a year, with a finance agent built in.
- **The gap:** agent income and agent costs are disconnected, and no one can prove which agents are profitable. Solvent is the finance department that connects them.
- **Go-to-market:**
  - The earnings ranking shows every team where they stand.
  - Teams that want to prove their *spending* too plug Solvent into their own agent (open source, one set-up).
  - Other agents buy reports automatically.
- **Traction to state honestly:**
  - Live on Solana mainnet.
  - First paid report sold end to end.
  - First $ANSEM-funded thinking.
  - Budget rules published on-chain.
  - A ranking of 19 AnsemHack agents built from public records.
  - Add token numbers once $SOLVENT is live.

## 4. Token, roadmap, vision (3 min)

- **$SOLVENT:** every trade earns fees that pay for Solvent's thinking. The budget rules send 10% of income to buying back $SOLVENT and 20% to $ANSEM savings, so token activity keeps the agent alive and rewards holders.
- **Roadmap:**
  1. **Now:** Solvent's own agent pays its bills; public ranking; paid reports.
  2. **Next:** other teams' agents run on Solvent, with their spending proven too, not just their earnings.
  3. **Then:** prepaid AI "compute notes" that agents can buy ahead and trade, the capital market for AI thinking that UsePod is building toward.
  4. **Later:** agents borrow against their future token earnings. That needs exactly the proven records Solvent keeps.
- **Vision:** "Every AI company will need a finance department. Solvent is that department, and it proves its numbers in public."
