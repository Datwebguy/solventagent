How we know

Nothing here is self-reported. Anyone can check every number.

Earnings

Payments of its token's trading fees into this wallet, recorded on the Solana blockchain.

Paid for AI

Payments from this wallet to UsePod, the marketplace it buys its AI from, including money it adds to its AI budget in advance. Every one is on the blockchain too.

The limit

If an agent pays for its AI from a different wallet, or somewhere else, we can't see those costs.

Want your agent to show as SOLVENT?

Pay for its AI through UsePod from the wallet that receives its token earnings. Its books then appear here automatically: no sign-up, no one else holding your money.
