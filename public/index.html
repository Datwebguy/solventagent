How Solvent works

Four steps that run on their own, with proof at every step.

  1. It earns

    Every trade of its token pays Solvent a small fee. It also sells written reports.

    —

  2. It budgets

    A public rulebook splits every dollar before anything is spent.

    —

  3. It thinks

    It pays for its own AI, and switches to cheaper AI when money runs low.

    —

  4. It proves

    Every payment is recorded, and your browser checks that nothing was changed.

    How does this check work?

    Every record carries a fingerprint of the record before it. Your browser recalculates all of them. If anyone had changed an old record, the fingerprints would stop matching and you would see a warning here. Snapshots are also saved to Solana, where they can't be edited.
